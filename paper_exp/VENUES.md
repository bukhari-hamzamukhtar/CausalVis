# Where to submit, cheapest first (rewritten 20 September 2026)

Publication and travel costs cannot be paid, so cost decides the order. One venue at a time
(no dual submission). An arXiv preprint is allowed alongside all of them.

## What each kind of venue actually costs
| venue kind | cost to publish | cost if accepted |
|---|---|---|
| arXiv preprint | free | nothing |
| TMLR, DMLR (journals) | free, no article charge | nothing; no registration, no travel |
| IEEE TPAMI, IJCV | free on the subscription route (open access is optional and expensive) | nothing |
| CVPR, NeurIPS, ICML, CLeaR, BMVC, WACV | free to submit | at least one author must register: roughly 500 to 1,000 USD at the student rate, plus travel |
| Workshops at those conferences | free to submit | registration if you present |
| Journals with an article charge (IEEE Access and similar) | 1,000 USD and up | avoid |

## Recommended path (no money needed at any step)
1. **arXiv** as soon as the paper is final. Free. First-time submitters in cs.CV need an
   endorsement from someone who has posted there; your supervisor can endorse you.
2. **TMLR** (Transactions on Machine Learning Research). Free to publish, no registration, no
   travel. Reviews ask two questions: are the claims supported by the evidence, and would some
   readers care. "Not novel enough" is not a valid reason to reject there, which fits this paper,
   because its contribution is measurement. Rolling submission, about two months to a decision.
   Roughly 10% of accepted papers get a certification that lets you present at NeurIPS, ICML or
   ICLR, and that is optional.
3. **DMLR** (Data-centric Machine Learning Research) if TMLR rejects. Free, and benchmark
   analysis is its core topic.
4. **A workshop** at CVPR, ICLR or NeurIPS (world models, physical reasoning, negative results).
   Most are non-archival, so the paper can still go to a journal afterwards. Only pay if you
   choose to attend.
5. **CVPR or ICML** only if you can cover registration or get a waiver. Both have student rates
   and limited financial assistance, and both need an application well before the deadline.
   CVPR 2027: paper registration 10 November 2026, paper 16 November 2026.

## Not worth it for this paper
- Journals that charge an article processing fee.
- Any venue that needs travel to be paid by you.
- National conferences with a registration fee: they cost money and add little to the record.
